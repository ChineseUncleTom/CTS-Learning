Lv.1 · analog vs digital waveforms

Two cats, one café (=^･ω･^=)

Anna sits in the sunbeam and her fur ripples continuously. Digi sits on the windowsill and only ever does one of two things: on, or off. Hold that picture and half the chapter is already done.

Anna Log
CONTINUOUS · SMOOTH
  • She's a dimmer switch — every position between off and full is available.
  • One unbroken flowing line, amplitude changing over time.
  • Analogous to a real physical thing: sound pressure, image brightness.
  • Weakness: she picks up every speck of dirt she walks past. Forever.
Digi Tal
DISCRETE · STEPPED
  • He's a ceiling-fan switch — speed 1, 2, 3. Nothing in between. Ever.
  • A row of rectangles snapping between high (1) and low (0).
  • Not a copy of the world — a list of numbers describing it.
  • Superpower: a smudged number is still readable. He never degrades.
Nnnh, I contain infinite values between off and on… every single one of them… it's exhausting, honestly (´｡• ᵕ •｡`)
I contain two. One is on. Zero is off. I sleep very well.
★ SKILL GET! Anna slides. Digi steps.

Analog = all the values in between. Digital = discrete, definite, just 1 and 0. If a question says "a signal with many varying states" — that's Anna, that's analog.

⚠ exam trap #1 In a digital signal one is ON and zero is OFF. This is literally question 1 and half the room still writes it backwards. Remember: 1 stands up. 0 is taking a nap.
Lv.2 · sampling & the Nyquist–Shannon theorem

How the café takes an order 📸

Anna hums a tune. Digi can't hear humming — he can only write numbers on a ticket. So he takes a snapshot of how loud she is, over and over, and writes each one down. That snapshot is a sample. How often he takes it is the sampling rate, in hertz.

If I snap fast enough, I can rebuild your whole song from dots alone. More than twice your highest note. That's the rule. Nya.
★ SKILL GET! Double the top, or the tune gets chopped.

Nyquist–Shannon: you can rebuild the signal only if you sample at more than twice the highest frequency present. Humans hear up to 20 kHz → you need more than 40 kHz → the CD uses 44.1 kHz. Blu-ray goes 48, 96 or 192 kHz.

Plenty of dots! Join them up and Anna's tune comes back perfectly.

🎬 nerd corner Why wagon wheels spin backwards in old films. The wheel turns faster than the camera grabs frames, so the frames tell a slower story than the truth. Same crime: you sampled too slowly. Digi calls it aliasing. Anna calls it lying.
Lv.3 · bit depth

How many crayons in the box 🖍

Sampling rate is how often Digi looks. Bit depth is how precisely he can describe what he saw. One bit gives him two words: on, off. Sixteen bits give him 65,536 words.

★ SKILL GET! Rate is the clock. Depth is the crayon box.

Rate → accuracy (did it catch the right frequency?). Depth → precision (did it catch the right level?). Every extra bit doubles the crayon box: 2n states.

Lv.4 · bit rate · bps vs Bps

How fast the beans pour ⚡

Bit rate is how much information flows per second, in bits per second. And it's just the two things you already know, multiplied together.

★ SKILL GET! Snaps × Shades × Speakers.

Snaps = sample rate. Shades = bit depth. Speakers = channels. CD audio: 44,100 × 16 × 2 = 1.411 Mbps. This is the one number worth having memorised walking into the exam.

Lv.6 · lossless vs lossy compression

Two ways to shrink a cake 🎂

Digi's tickets are getting long. One uncompressed HD frame is about 6.2 MB; thirty of those a second is roughly 11 GB a minute. Something has to give.

LOSSLESS
FOLD IT · NOTHING THROWN AWAY

Spot repeated patterns, store the pattern once, then rebuild the original perfectly. Smaller savings, zero loss.

  • ZIP · FLAC · PNG · MPEG-2 · JPEG2000
  • The only acceptable choice for text and financial transactions.
LOSSY
EAT THE CRUMBS NOBODY NOTICES

Exploits the fact that your brain can't perceive every detail anyway and throws that detail away. Huge savings, real loss.

  • MP3 · JPEG · HEVC · H.264 (AVC)
  • Perfect for audio, video and images. Never for a spreadsheet.
★ SKILL GET! Lossless for Ledgers. Lossy for Lazy senses.

If a human eye or ear is the final judge, you can cheat it → lossy. If a number is the final judge (text, money, spreadsheets), you may not lose a single bit → lossless. Question 4 is exactly this, and the answer is "audio, video, and images."

🔢 the number trick Plain JPEG is lossy — but JPEG2000 is lossless. Plain MPEG is lossy — but MPEG-2 sits on the lossless list. Add a number, keep the detail. This is the only part of those two lists people actually get wrong.

Lv.7 · digital media formats

The cup and the recipe ☕

The single most confusing pair in the chapter — and in a café it's completely obvious.

container

The cup ☕

The structure the data sits in. It also holds the metadata — the little label on the side saying how the drink is arranged and which recipe was used.

AVI = Audio Video Interleaved. It's a cup. It doesn't brew anything.

codec

The recipe 📝

The method for encoding (compressing) and decoding (decompressing) what goes in the cup. Sometimes it must be installed on the machine before it can pour.

CODEC = COder + DECoder. The word literally contains its own definition.

★ SKILL GET! An AVI cup can hold an MPEG brew.

One container, many possible codecs inside — which is exactly why people mix the two words up, and why some names refer to both. VLC is famous because it knows a huge number of recipes.

I once ordered a latte in a soup bowl. Technically valid. The container never said no. …That's your whole memory of "the container is just the structure."
Lv.8 · noise, transmission & generation loss

The photocopy cat 📠 (╥﹏╥)

Here's the story that carries the last three exam questions.

🎨Anna posts a painting.

📮

Anna paints her own portrait and posts it across the country. Every few hundred kilometres a tired clerk photocopies it and sends the copy onward — because the paint keeps fading, and the copier is the only way to make it bold again.

☕

But the copier faithfully enlarges the coffee ring, the smudge, and the dust on the glass too. After enough re-amplification, the portrait is the smudges.

📉

That's an analog signal down a long cable: every repeater amplifier boosts the noise along with the signal, until the two are indistinguishable.

🔢Digi posts a list of numbers.

📋

Digi doesn't post a painting at all. He posts a list of numbers. His page gets exactly as grubby as Anna's — coffee ring and all.

🧐

But the clerk doesn't photocopy it. The clerk reads it, decides whether each mark was meant to be high or low, and types a clean new page. Coffee ring gone. ✨

♾️

That's why simple processing circuitry can regenerate a digital signal with the noise stripped off — and why the hundredth copy is identical to the first.

★ SKILL GET! A smudged 7 is still a 7.

And the analog half: every copy catches a cold. Warped tape, scratched disc, shed oxide, one more generation of duplication — analog gets worse every time and never recovers. Digital copying is just re-reading numbers, so quality never moves.

The cheat sheet 📋 ✧

  1. Anna slides, Digi steps. Analog = continuous, many varying states. Digital = discrete on/off.
  2. One is on, zero is off. Always. Question 1, every time.
  3. Double the top, or the tune gets chopped. Nyquist: sample above 2× the highest frequency. 20 kHz hearing → 44.1 kHz CD.
  4. Rate is the clock, depth is the crayon box. Rate → accuracy. Depth → precision.
  5. Bit depth = number of STATES, and it's 2ⁿ. 1-bit = 2, 4-bit = 16, 8-bit = 256, 16-bit = 65,536 = CD.
  6. Snaps × Shades × Speakers. 44,100 × 16 × 2 = 1.411 Mbps.
  7. Big B, big bite — 8 bits per byte. Bps → bps is ×8.
  8. Lossless for Ledgers (ZIP, FLAC, PNG, MPEG-2, JPEG2000) — text and money only.
    Lossy for Lazy senses (MP3, JPEG, HEVC, H.264/AVC) — audio, video, images.
  9. Add a number, keep the detail. JPEG lossy → JPEG2000 lossless.
  10. The cup and the recipe. Container = structure + metadata (AVI). Codec = COder + DECoder.
  11. A smudged 7 is still a 7 (digital regenerates clean) — but every copy catches a cold (analog noise compounds with every re-amplification and every generation).
